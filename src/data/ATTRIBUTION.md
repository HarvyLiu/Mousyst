# English vocabulary

Derived from wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq.
Vocabulary data is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/.
This subset retains the first 20,000 ASCII alphabetic English tokens in frequency order.
It is generated by tools/build_vocabulary.py. Frequency order is retained; exact frequencies are omitted.

Wordfreq incorporates freely available SUBTLEX data by Marc Brysbaert,
Boris New, Emmanuel Keuleers and colleagues, OpenSubtitles data, Wikipedia,
Google Books Ngrams, and other sources. See the accompanying source notices.
SUBTLEX remains freely available at https://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies.
Full source attribution: https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq#license.
